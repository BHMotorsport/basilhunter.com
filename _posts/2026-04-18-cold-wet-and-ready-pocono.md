BH Motorsport kicked off the 2026 season at Pocono Raceway in April, running the South/East layout. The weather did not cooperate. Cold air and steady rain kept the surface slick all day and put a premium on smooth inputs and patience.

Those are the days that teach you the most. The Porsche Cayman GTS felt planted and predictable even as the track stayed wet, and its balanced mid-engine layout paid off through the slower, technical sections of the course. The car ran clean from the first session to the last with no drama.

It was not the glamour opener anyone hoped for, but it was a productive one. BH Motorsport left Pocono with good data, more wet-weather seat time, and a car that proved it can handle whatever the calendar throws at it.
