June brought a complete turnaround. BH Motorsport headed to New Jersey Motorsports Park's Lightning circuit over Father's Day weekend and got the kind of weather every driver dreams about. Warm, dry, and clear from the first session to the last.

Lightning is a tight 1.9 mile course that rewards precision over horsepower, and the Cayman GTS was in its element. The car turned in sharply, settled quickly, and put its power down cleanly out of the slow corners. Grip stayed strong all weekend and the GTS ran flawlessly session after session.

Great weather, a happy car, and a holiday weekend at the track. It does not get much better than that.
